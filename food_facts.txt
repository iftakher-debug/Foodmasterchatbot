Foodmaster food facts
umami: Umami is a savoury taste often found in tomatoes, mushrooms, seaweed and aged cheese.
fermentation: Fermentation uses microorganisms such as yeasts or bacteria to change food. It is used to make yoghurt, kimchi and sourdough.
al dente: Al dente is an Italian term for pasta that is cooked but still slightly firm when bitten.
roux: A roux is a mixture of flour and fat cooked together to thicken sauces, soups and gravies.
tempering spices: Tempering spices means briefly heating spices in oil or fat to release their aroma. Take care because they can burn quickly.
mise en place: Mise en place is a French cooking term that means preparing and organising ingredients before cooking.
